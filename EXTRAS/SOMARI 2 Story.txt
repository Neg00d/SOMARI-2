One fateful morning, Dreed — the infamous German Sonic YouTuber known for his wild retro gaming experiments — had a brilliantly stupid idea. For his next YouTube challenge, he decided to do something no one had dared before: plug both a weird looking copy of Sonic the Hedgehog Master System aaaaand a original Super Mario Bros. NES cartridge into his toaster.

Yes,... his toaster!

He claimed it would “unlock the full power of 8-bit nostalgia.” Viewers in the live chat were going wild. “This is peak content,” someone wrote. “Bro’s gonna invent fire again,” said another.

The moment he pulled the toaster lever down, a blinding blue-red flash burst from the slots. The lights in his room flickered. His cat named Miku (aka. his only real friend) ran out of the room. His camera glitched.

And suddenly — FIVE BIG BOOMS?! BOOM, BOOM, BOOM,.. you get the idea — the screen turned black.

When it came back on, Dreed was no longer in his room. He was standing in the middle of a corrupted Mushroom Kingdom, where Goombas looked like trash, and pipes that ... uhm … they are just less detailed tbh.

Dreed: "Where the heck am I?!"

???: "Is this your first time here, duuh?"

Standing across from him, arms crossed and oozing attitude,... was SOMARI?! — The forbidden hybrid of Mario’s design and Sonic’s speed. “You broke the multicartridge barrier, dude,” Somari said with a smirk. “Now the Mushroom Kingdom's glitching out, and someone’s gotta save it duuuuh.”

Dreed grabbed his camera, still recording somehow. “Let’s do this together man.”

But Somari raised a finger. “No no no ... bro … (DRAMATIC PAUSE) we settle this the cool way duuuuuuuuuuuh.”

Suddenly, he whipped out a hand and challenged Dreed to the ultimate decision-maker: Rock, Paper, Scissors.

Three rounds! One Mission! Only one hero!

Dreed took a deep breath. “Alright, you blue-booted imposter plumber. Let’s go.”

Rock. Paper. Scissors. SHOOT!!!

The fate of the Mushroom Kingdom — and the YouTube algorithm — hung in the balance.
